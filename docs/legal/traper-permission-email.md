**Subject:** Permission request — use of Traper product images and 2026 catalogue in the app "Upecaj!" (Serbia)

---

Dear Sir or Madam,

my name is Mihailo Janković. I am the developer of **Upecaj!**, a free Android
application for anglers in Serbia — no ads, no subscriptions, no user accounts.
I am writing through your Serbian importer, m-fishing.rs, who kindly agreed to
forward this request.

The app rates fishing conditions for 795 Serbian waters and, for each water and
season, recommends a concrete groundbait recipe. All 80 curated recipes are built
around Traper products, because Traper is what Serbian feeder anglers actually
use. Every product shown in the app links directly to m-fishing.rs, so the app
sends buyers straight to your importer. I earn nothing from the app.

Before publishing on Google Play, I would like your written permission for the
following. A short "yes" by e-mail for each point is all I need — I am not asking
for a contract or for any exclusive rights, only non-exclusive, royalty-free,
revocable permission for this app and its promotion, with Traper credited as the
source throughout.

1. **Product photographs** — may I use official Traper product images in the app
   and in its Google Play store listing? If you have a press or media kit for
   dealers, I would prefer to use those files rather than the photographs
   currently taken from your importer's shop pages.

2. **Traper Catalogue 2026 (PDF)** — may I make it available inside the app?
   Alternatively, if you host the PDF on your own server, I can simply link to it
   instead of including the file, which suits me technically just as well.

3. **Brand and product names** — may I use the Traper name and your product names
   in descriptive text in the app? If you have brand guidelines, please send them
   and I will follow them.

4. **Official groundbait recipes** — your website publishes recipes with exact
   proportions. May I use those ratios in the app, credited to Traper? At the
   moment my recipes are marked as my own estimates. Official proportions from the
   manufacturer, combined with my own mapping to specific Serbian rivers and
   lakes, would make the app considerably more useful to anglers here — and it
   would point them precisely at the Traper products they need.

I would be glad to send you a test build or a short video walkthrough if you would
like to see the app before deciding.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
